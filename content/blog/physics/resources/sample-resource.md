## Database for Semiconductors

Physical properties of semiconductors: band, optical, electrical, mechanical and thermal parameters; an n, k database for A3B5, A2B6, metals, oxides and silicides; heterostructure properties and models; and fundamental physical constants. #not updated

[Ioffe Institute semiconductor database](https://www.ioffe.ru/SVA/)


## Optica Open

Preprint server through the Optica Publishing Group that hosts submissions dedicated to advancing optics and photonics. 

[Optica Preprints](https://opticaopen.org/figshare)

## Refractive Index Database

This database contains RI information for all important materials. It also has a section where the results can be graphically visualized.

[Refractive Index Database](https://refractiveindex.info/)
