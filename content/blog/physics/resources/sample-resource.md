## QC Lab

QC Lab is a Python package for the simulation of quantum–classical dynamics, offering a high degree of cross-compatibility between quantum–classical algorithms and models.

[QC Lab documentation](https://tempelaar-team.github.io/qclab/interactive_docs/index.html)

## Mobile Python Challenge

In collaboration with Science in Society, we are developing the Mobile Python Challenge, an introductory course in Python programming. By means of a series of "escape room"-like challenges driven by a narrative, we aim to present an engaging and playful course that fosters excitement about coding among early high-schoolers.

[Tempelaar Group — Northwestern](https://tempelaar.ci.northwestern.edu/)

## Explore Chemistry

A collection from the American Chemical Society: the periodic table, Adventures in Chemistry, the Reactions video series, chemical history, What's That Stuff? on everyday products, Reel Science, the Tiny Matters podcast, and more.

[ACS — What is Chemistry?](https://www.acs.org/education/whatischemistry.html)

## Database for Semiconductors

Physical properties of semiconductors: band, optical, electrical, mechanical and thermal parameters; an n, k database for A3B5, A2B6, metals, oxides and silicides; heterostructure properties and models; and fundamental physical constants.

[Ioffe Institute semiconductor database](https://www.ioffe.ru/SVA/)
