## Meet Mars

{{% level "kids" %}}
Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun, and it's red! Its ground is covered
in rusty dust, the same kind of rust you find on an old bike. Mars is
smaller than Earth, much colder, and you couldn't breathe its air.
{{% /level %}}

{{% level "students scholars" %}}
Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun, at an average distance of about
1.5 times Earth's. It is roughly half Earth's diameter, and its distinctive
colour comes from iron oxide (rust) in the dust covering its surface.
Its thin atmosphere is about 95% carbon dioxide, with a surface pressure
less than 1% of Earth's.
{{% /level %}}

{{% level "scholars" %}}
Mars has about 11% of Earth's mass and an orbital eccentricity of about
0.09, much larger than Earth's, so its distance from the Sun varies
noticeably over a year. Its axial tilt of about 25° is similar to Earth's,
so Mars has seasons. They are roughly twice as long as ours and strongly
affected by that eccentric orbit.
{{% /level %}}
