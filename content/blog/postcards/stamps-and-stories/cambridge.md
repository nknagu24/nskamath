## On the card

> Cambridge in July — all spires and bicycles and a rain that never quite commits. A good place to remember why the questions matter.

## About this card

This one came home with me from a conference in the summer of 2025 — my first time in the city whose name I'd read on the spine of a hundred papers. The front is King's College Chapel at dusk...

The stamp is nothing rare — an ordinary Machin definitive — but I like that it carries the everyday postal life of the city...
