## **What is Gin Rummy?**

Gin Rummy is a two-player variant of Rummy. The core objective is simple: arrange your cards into **melds** (matching sets) faster and more efficiently than your opponent, while minimizing unmatched cards called **deadwood**.

### **Card Point Values**

| Card | Value |
|------|-------|
| Ace (A) | 1 point |
| 2-9 | Face value |
| 10, J, Q, K | 10 points |

*Example: ♠3 + ♦9 + ♥K = 3 + 9 + 10 = 22 points*


<div class="highlight-rule">
<strong>Dealing:</strong> Each player is dealt 10 cards one at a time. The 21st card is placed face-up as the <strong>discard pile</strong>, and the remaining deck becomes the <strong>stock pile</strong>.
</div>

**Objective:** Be the first to form your hand into valid melds, bringing your deadwood (unmatched cards) to zero or getting **knocked**.


## **Important Terminologies**

{{< concept title="Meld" >}}
***Three or more cards of the same rank or in sequence.***

**Example:**

1. **Sets (Same Rank)**

Three or more cards of the same rank (any suit):
- ♠7, ♥7, ♦7 (valid set)
- ♠K, ♥K, ♦K, ♣K (valid set of four)

2. **Sequences (Same Suit, Consecutive)**

Three or more cards of the same suit in consecutive order:
- ♠4, ♠5, ♠6 (valid sequence)
- ♥10, ♥J, ♥Q, ♥K (valid sequence)
{{< /concept >}}

**Important:** 
- Melds must have at least 3 cards
- Sequences cannot wrap around (King-Ace-2 is NOT valid)
- A card can only be used once in your hand


{{< concept title="Deadwood" >}}
Unmatched cards in your hand.
**Example hand:**
- Melds: ♠5-♠6-♠7 (sequence), ♥K-♦K-♣K (set)
- Unmatched: ♠3, ♦9, ♥2
- Deadwood = 3 + 9 + 2 = **14 points**
{{< /concept >}}

## **Turn Structure**

{{< concept title="Opening Move" >}}
The **non-dealer player** starts. They can either:
- Take the upturned card (discard pile) and discard one card, OR
- Pass without taking or discarding

If they pass, the **dealer** gets the same choice. If the dealer takes the upturned card, they discard. If they also pass, then the non-dealer takes the top card from the stock deck.

After this opening, normal play begins: each player takes from either the stock or discard pile, then discards.
{{< /concept >}}

On your turn, you **must do exactly two things in order:**

{{< concept title="Step 1: Draw" >}}
Take one card from either:
- The **stock pile** (face-down), OR
- The **discard pile** (face-up)

You must draw every turn, even if you're building Gin.
{{< /concept >}}

{{< concept title="Step 2: Discard" >}}
Place one card face-up on the discard pile.

This ends your turn and passes play to your opponent.
{{< /concept >}}

## **Knocking and Gin**

{{< concept title="Knocking" >}}
When your deadwood count reaches **10 or fewer points**, you can knock:
1. Lay down all your melds face-up
2. Place your deadwood cards separately
3. Your opponent then does the same
{{< /concept >}}
Your opponent can then **lay off** cards onto your melds (add cards that continue sequences or sets).

**Scoring after knock:**
- If opponent's deadwood ≤ your deadwood: Opponent scores the difference + 10 point undercut bonus
- If opponent's deadwood > your deadwood: You score the difference
- If you knock and opponent ties: Opponent gets 10 point bonus



{{< concept title="Going Gin" >}}
If you have **zero deadwood** (all 10 cards are in melds):
- You've gone **Gin**
- You immediately win the hand
- Score 25 points + opponent's deadwood count (no lay-offs allowed)
{{< /concept >}}

This is the best possible outcome!



## **Deadwood Calculation**

Deadwood is the total point value of unmatched cards in your hand.

## **Winning the Game**

**Score tracking:**
- After each hand, the winner scores points
- First player to 100 points wins the game

## **Final Thoughts**

Gin Rummy rewards both tactical thinking and observation. Every card matters, and one good draw can transform your entire hand. It's a game that's easy to learn but takes skill to master — you'll find yourself playing "just one more hand" all evening.

The beauty of Gin Rummy is that it's fast (usually 15-30 minutes per game), requires minimal setup, and works perfectly for two players who want a challenging, strategic card game.
